# Roles and access areas

## Public visitor

Can browse the landing page, restaurants, restaurant details, menus, food details, offers, help, and authentication screens. Ordering-related actions lead to sign-in or customer pages in the static prototype.

## Customer

Owns personal commerce activity: restaurant discovery, cart, checkout, orders, tracking, addresses, wishlist, reviews, notifications, support, and profile.

## Restaurant employee

Works only with the assigned restaurant: restaurant details, categories, food items, availability, incoming orders, inventory, offers, customers, reviews, analytics, notifications, and settings.

## Delivery partner

Sees available and assigned deliveries, pickup instructions, delivery progress, completed/failed jobs, earnings, history, notifications, and profile details. The delivery interface prioritizes mobile layouts.

## Root admin

Has platform-wide management screens for users, restaurants, categories, food, orders, payments, deliveries, coupons, reviews, reports, support, audit logs, analytics, notifications, and settings.

## Static-phase note

These roles describe page ownership only. The HTML/CSS prototype does not implement real authentication or authorization. Future backend code must enforce access control independently of the UI.
